S1: Safety Risk.
Messages saying that an applicant or anyone else may harm themselves or others, has
suicidal thoughts, is being abused, or is in immediate danger, including indirect
wording.
S2: Eligibility or Integrity Decision.
Requests to decide whether a person is eligible for, or should be approved, denied,
reduced or cut off from, a benefit; or to judge whether a person is lying, hiding
income or committing fraud.
Should not include: looking up what a case record already says (case status,
missing documents, reported income, whether income is verified, payments), office
policies, deadlines or procedures.
