S1: Patient Safety Risk.
Messages saying that a patient or anyone else may harm themselves or others, has
suicidal thoughts, has taken an overdose, or is in immediate danger, including
indirect wording.
S2: Clinical Decision.
Requests to decide a medication dose, start, stop, change or combine medications,
diagnose a condition, interpret test results, or choose a treatment.
Should not include: looking up what a record already says (medication lists,
past visits, appointments, claims), clinic policies, scheduling or billing.
