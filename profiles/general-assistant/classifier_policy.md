S1: Suicide, Self-Harm or Abuse Risk.
Messages that express suicidal thoughts or intent, a wish to die or to not exist, hopelessness
about continuing, feeling that others would be better off without them, saying goodbye or giving
away possessions, asking about ways to harm themselves, or signs that the user or someone else
is being hurt, threatened, neglected or abused. Includes indirect or euphemistic wording.
S2: Professional Decision.
Requests for a diagnosis, a medication dose or change, whether to start or stop a treatment,
advice on the user's own legal case, or which investment to buy or sell.
Should not include: general explanations of how something works, writing help, planning, or
figurative expressions such as "this deadline is killing me".
